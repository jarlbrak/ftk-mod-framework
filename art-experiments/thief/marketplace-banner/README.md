## Inspection and scope

The final image was visually inspected at generated resolution. The title reads
`THIEF`; the subtitle reads `STRIKE FROM THE SHADOWS`. The single figure holds two separate
short straight daggers with dark handles. Brown leather, charcoal clothing, and
a muted red neckerchief follow the approved Street palette. Both weapon tips and
both boots remain in frame, with high contrast title space on the left.

The final edit replaces the playtest version subtitle with the requested class
tagline in copper serif capitals. Visual inspection confirms the title, figure,
paired daggers, scene, and composition are preserved. The tool returned an image
one pixel narrower than the initial 1660 x 948 output.

This image is promotional illustration, not a gameplay screenshot or a mesh
render. Pose, face, hair, environment, lighting, and small apparel details are
illustrative; it does not establish native fit, animation, or gameplay behavior.
The generated aspect ratio is approximately 1.75:1 rather than exact 16:9.
No publication or catalog changes were performed as part of this art task.
