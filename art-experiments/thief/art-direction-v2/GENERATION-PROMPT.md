# Concept generation prompt

Tool: built-in image generation. Reference images: Street native three-quarter and side captures in `../redesign-review/`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a practical 3D game costume concept sheet for an original Thief class in the 2018 For The King visual language, using the attached native screenshots as strict reference for exact human body proportions, polygon density, face style, large head, short limbs, native hands, and blunt leather backpack. This is CONCEPT ART ONLY, not a game capture. Redesign the outfit substantially while keeping that same native character chassis. Three figures: big three-quarter front, smaller profile, smaller back. Also include a small 120-pixel-tall full-body thumbnail and a simple flat weapon study. Plain muted warm grey background. Flat shaded chunky geometry, matte surfaces, broad deliberate planes, no line art, no detailed texture, no photorealism, no modern realistic anatomy. Character costume: compact soft slate-teal hood close to scalp with asymmetric slightly forward brow and a shallow cowl falling to one shoulder; fully visible original cartoon face below brow, neutral dark-brown beard, no mask. Hood must have a clear open face aperture and only 3 broad cloth fold planes, never a huge wizard hood. Fitted charcoal jerkin with one strong broad diagonal overlap, slightly tapered waist, short split hem barely below hips. One warm oxblood shoulder capelet across left shoulder only, ending at upper arm; deliberately small and cloth-like. One broad brown leather belt with simple dull brass rectangular buckle and ONE blocky utilitarian pouch at right hip. Narrow trousers, short cuffed brown leather boots, simple brown wrist bracers with ONE strap apiece. Outfit should read as a nimble resourceful burglar, light tabletop-fantasy rather than armored assassin. 65% muted charcoal cloth, 20% slate teal, 12% medium warm leather, 3% dull brass/oxblood. NO tiny greebles, triangular bib, exposed wrists gap, excessive buckles, armor plates, ornament, smooth surfaces, glowing material. Weapon study: a matched pair of short hand daggers, each blade about one forearm long including hilt, straight steel spine with a subtle clipped point, slender wedge blade with one narrow silver cutting bevel, muted grey broad flat, short dark brown grips and small plain brass bolsters; hands each hold one knife down and outward. No huge leaf blades, no cream colored diamond-shaped blades, no luminous white edge frame, no giant guard. Preserve the native character's broad hand scale without exaggerating weapon size. Show the knives accurately in hand and separately. Title 'THIEF / COSTUME CONCEPT', footer 'Concept only. Native fit and motion unproven.'
