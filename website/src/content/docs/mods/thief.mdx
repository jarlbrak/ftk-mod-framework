## Find an opening

Thief is a fast specialist who starts with **Street Twins**, **Patched Jack**, **Street Neckerchief**, **Softstep Shoes** and **Lockpicks**. Paired daggers use Speed; bows use Awareness. Sneak, Ambush and Trap Disarm support exploration. The published 1.0.0 collection contains 45 equipment pieces, including three artifact weapons, across seven tiers.

## Openings and preparation

**Sneak Attack** adds 20% of current maximum weapon damage to a perfect basic Strike or Shoot against an **Open** enemy, or while **Prepared**, before native modifiers and armor. An enemy is Open before its first scheduled turn, or after a different ally damages it directly and before its next turn. Your own damage does not create an opening. The qualifying attempt spends the once-per-own-turn opportunity even with partial rolls; partial results receive ordinary damage.

**Prepared** lets the next eligible basic precision attack treat its target as Open. It is spent on the attempt, cannot stack, and expires at the end of your next turn, on weapon change, incapacity or combat exit.

**Twin Feint** grants Prepared when a paired-dagger basic Strike misses exactly one check and causes HP damage, once per own turn. Two visible blades resolve as one damage event. Bows do not receive this passive.

**Slip Away** spends your full turn, once per combat, to grant Prepared and halve the next damaging direct enemy attack before your next turn. It costs no Focus and requires no roll. It does not stop damage over time or attached conditions, and its reduction does not multiply with Paladin Guard.

## Dagger and bow actions

**Feint** and **Draw Out** deal 0.60× weapon damage; a hit causing HP loss grants Prepared to a Thief. Their Locksmith versions deal 0.80×. Other classes receive the weapon action's damage without the Thief preparation effect.

**Pierce** and **Thread the Needle** deal 0.75× weapon damage and ignore Armor on a perfect result. Their Wayfarer versions deal 0.85×. Partial results still face full Armor. These special actions cannot Sneak Attack; each weapon also retains its basic attack.

For example, a perfect eligible basic attack with current weapon damage 20 has a damage basis of 24 before criticals, Armor and other native modifiers. This is arithmetic, not a captured combat result. A damaging Feint can prepare a later target; Slip Away can prepare without harming anyone. Neither gives an extra attack.

## Artifacts

**The Skeleton Key** is a paired-dagger weapon. A damaging Sneak Attack returns one Focus actually spent on that attack, capped by current capacity. Spending no Focus returns none; partial or fully absorbed hits and Pierce do not qualify. Keep the weapon equipped through resolution.

**Candle's End** is a paired-dagger weapon. Once per combat, an eligible basic Strike against a full-health enemy replaces the ordinary +20% Sneak Attack bonus with +75% on a perfect result. The qualifying attempt spends the charge even on a partial or absorbed hit. Weapon swaps and revival do not refresh it.

**The Unlost Road** is a bow. A damaging Sneak Attack grants +8 Evasion points until your next turn. The effect cannot stack and ends on weapon change, incapacity or combat exit. Partial or absorbed hits and Thread the Needle grant nothing; this is not a guaranteed dodge.

All three can be equipped by other classes, but their signature effects require the Thief capability. They occupy both hands and do not require matching armor.

## Find your equipment

Street and Burglar lead into Guild and Masterwork. Locksmith favors preparation, Nightblade favors sustained damage and Wayfarer offers stronger penetration. The published bow collection does not declare matching-armor role effects or Guild Token exchange offers.

Regular equipment can appear through native drops, town markets, Night Markets and dungeon merchants according to its listed loot band. Artifacts exclude town markets. Eligibility is not a guaranteed spawn or an equip requirement. No Lore Store unlock is declared.

The library banner is promotional artwork, not gameplay. Balance, higher-tier and female outfit fit, and final mouse usability remain under review for published 1.0.0. Save/resume and hot-reload lifecycle are not verified for this published package. Start a new single-player adventure for testing and retain its exact mod set when returning to the save.
