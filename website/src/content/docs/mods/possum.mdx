## A different kind of adventurer

Choose Possum alongside the native character appearances. The original faceted model has a pointed muzzle, rounded ears, smaller palms and fingers, and a long pink tail. Your class abilities, stats, and native equipment remain yours.

The tail follows the body and has no independent animation. The package includes fourteen native class bindings, but that is broader than the observed gameplay coverage: the final smaller-hand model was verified in Hunter character selection, the overworld, and an ordinary starting-bow attack on macOS.

Other classes, armor changes, weapon animations, hit and death animations, save/reload, and online co-op are not established by that test. Restart after installing or removing the package.
