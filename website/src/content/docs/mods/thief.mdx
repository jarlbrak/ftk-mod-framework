## Find an opening

Thief is a fast, Speed-based specialist with two-handed paired daggers and bows. Sneak Attack rewards openings and preparation; Slip Away spends a turn and reports when it has been used. Version 1.0.0 tunes the ordinary Sneak Attack bonus to 20%.

The published playtest includes 45 equipment pieces across seven tiers, including Skeleton Key, Candle's End, and Unlost Road. Treat this as a chance to try the class and give feedback: balance, higher-tier and female outfit fit, and final mouse usability remain under review.

The library banner is a promotional illustration, not a gameplay capture. Start a new single-player adventure for testing, and keep its exact mod set if you intend to return to the save. Save/resume and hot-reload lifecycle are not yet verified for this package.
