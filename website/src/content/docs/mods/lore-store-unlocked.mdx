## The whole Lore Store, open

While this mod is installed, every Lore Store entry you could buy reads as purchased: classes, items, encounters, locations, and cosmetics. That includes entries from the free DLC packs, such as Cyclops Armor, Ninja Armor, Goblin Skin, Halloween Masks, and Dynasty Armor, even if you have not claimed them on your store page.

Some entries keep their real state. Paid DLC you do not own, such as Lost Civilization, stays locked. Entries the game hides from the store stay hidden, and limited-time cloud promotions unlock only while the game currently offers them. Encounters that reveal new Lore Store entries still appear, because reveals are left as genuine progress.

## Your own unlocks are untouched

The mod writes no Lore Store purchase to your profile or to Steam. It only changes the game's answer when it asks whether an entry is purchased, and only while the mod is loaded. Removing the mod restores exactly the unlocks you had, with no backup or restore step.

Lore points you earn while it is installed are real and stay yours to spend after removal. Achievements and statistics earned in play are recorded as usual. Resetting lore from the game's options still resets your real progress. Restart the game after installing or removing the mod.

## What has been verified

Requires framework 1.3.0 or a later 1.x release. The mod is published for Windows, macOS, and Linux on the listed game build. It was verified on macOS. On an isolated game copy seeded with a real profile, the store showed every non-DLC and free DLC entry as owned with the mod, the original locks returned after removal, and the Lore Store purchase records were unchanged. A macOS Steam installation also showed every store entry as owned.

Windows and Linux gameplay, unlocked items appearing in shops and loot during a run, and online co-op are unverified. By design, most co-op unlocks such as classes and item pools apply only to the player who has the mod, and the game shares some world unlocks with the party, but this has not been tested. Removing the mod during an adventure keeps that run's world and inventory, but later shops, loot, and encounters follow your real purchases again.
