Six families, eight pieces per family, and three legendary artifacts. Regular pieces are eligible for drops, town markets, Night Markets, and dungeon merchants. Artifacts exclude town markets. Loot bands do not guarantee availability; base gold is a declared value, not a guaranteed shop price.

Every Paladin hammer grants Censure. Every Paladin trinket grants Smite while equipped. Guardian perks only apply to a Guardian class. Fractional Speed and Vitality modifiers are shown as character stat points below. Zero modifiers are retained so the table does not imply an undeclared defense bonus.
