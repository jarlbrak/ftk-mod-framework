# Thief visual inspiration

Research and design proposal, 2026-09-23. This brief supplements [DIRECTION.md](DIRECTION.md) and the [equipment art brief](../../../docs/thief/ART-DIRECTION.md). It proposes original art, with no changes to mechanics, stats, production meshes, or package assets. Linked commercial images are viewing references only.

## Recommended next study

Make Street a resourceful travelling burglar with bare native hair, a compact low neckerchief, a fitted leather jerkin, one tool pouch, and two practical knives. The scarf should sit below the mouth in the first study. The expressive native face remains the focal point; a small cloth accent and useful equipment carry the profession. Test a partially raised scarf only as a separate variant after the open face passes review.

This revises the earlier headwear study: repeated cap and hood iterations have struggled to look like cloth at native proportions. A new head shell is not required for the next Street comparison. Later bands can explore a lowered cowl or compact open hood when a specific silhouette and fitting benefit justify it.

## Shape, color and weapon targets

- **Head:** native hair silhouette and visible eyebrows, eyes and mouth. One low folded scarf with a short tucked end. Avoid a circular crown band or inflated shell.
- **Body:** two or three broad leather panels taper into one belt. Keep cloth continuous at the elbows and wrists. One pouch and one asymmetric shoulder fold provide enough story at this scale.
- **Palette:** start with slate cloth, warm brown leather, a restrained oxblood scarf and dull brass buckle. Keep cloth and leather distinguishable in grayscale. These are original art choices, not sampled faction colors.
- **Weapons:** compact paired blades with a straight spine, small clipped point, dark grip and mid-value steel face. Limit the bright metal to a thin cutting edge. Keep the pair visually below sword size and avoid luminous borders or oversized guards.
- **Progression:** Street gains character from repair and practical pockets; Guild gains cleaner tailoring and a deliberate short split hem; Nightblade gains an angled collar and reduced trim. Each needs a geometry change that survives a grayscale thumbnail.

## Evidence limits and acceptance

The observed Defias and Syndicate screenshots support those individual appearances. They do not prove faction-wide uniform rules, exact color standards, or every Classic NPC variation. The linked user-created [Syndicate Rogue outfit](https://www.wowhead.com/outfit=607586/syndicate-rogue) is optional mood reference only, not canonical faction evidence. BG3 Rogue armor and Astarion are separate references; neither establishes a unique required Thief subclass costume.

Review Street first at 96 and 160 pixels character height beside the current native outfit. Require a recognizable native face, a clear waist, continuous sleeves, a visible tool pouch and two readable knives. Compare front, profile and rear in identical lighting. A scarf should not merge into a rigid chest plate or conceal a fitting fault.

This is an art brief only. Model, renderer, skin/hair visibility and neck-motion support remain implementation dependencies to verify on the actual native avatar. No new attachment slot, animation, power budget, co-op behavior or live-game acceptance is implied.
