# Paladin equipment art audit

The 2026-09-21 review supports retaining the current six-set visual direction.
The inspected images show a plain starter and a readable progression into
ceremonial guardian equipment. No specific defect found in these samples warrants
another geometry revision. This is an art-direction assessment, not complete
motion, race-fit or user acceptance. The [audit receipt](art-acceptance-audit.json)
pins the reviewed local images and current source manifests.

## Visual findings

| Set | Observed identity | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Novice | Oxblood fitted jack, cream short sleeves, plain iron cap and continuous brown travel boots | Appropriately modest starter clothing. The torso has a defined waist; it does not repeat the rejected oversized belly, gold breastplate or floating toe ornaments. |
| Oathkeeper | Short blue field coat, split steel chest plates, small shoulder shells and partial greaves | A clear first step into armor while retaining exposed cloth and leather. |
| Highward | Complete framed breastplate, layered shoulders, long blue tabard and plated insteps | Distinct from Oathkeeper through coverage and garment length, not only recoloring. |
| Mercy | Broad ivory mantle, long rounded prayer panels, subdued bronze bindings and ivory gaiters | Cloth-led compassionate guardian identity. |
| Censure | Overlapping dark chest chevrons, compact shoulder plates, pointed red panels and tall folded greaves | Angular martial identity distinct from Mercy and Verdict. |
| Verdict | Bright heraldic breastplate, layered hip plates, navy tabard and low gold coronet | Structured ceremonial identity with a clear open face. |

The boots read as complete footwear with soles and connected ankle construction.
Later armor uses plate, cloth, heraldry and gold accents consistent with the
agreed Classic-inspired direction while keeping FTK proportions and native faces.
The equipment generators and package validation identify newly authored surfaces;
this audit makes no claim of importing Blizzard geometry or textures.

The twelve hammer designs retain the recognizable reference features the user
liked: compact framed or waisted starter heads, heavier bound early-tier heads,
Mercy's opposing steel hooks and blue wells, Censure's angular teeth, and
Verdict's red-and-gold spiked barrel. The six shields share an order emblem and
heater outline, with increasing trim and branch-specific gems, braces or fins.
Their shared silhouette is coherent with a progression family. The reviewed
weapon sheet is an offline study and does not prove hand placement in motion.

## Native scope and remaining evidence

Native bodies, faces, hair, backpacks and the seven inherited appearance entries
remain intentional. Custom equipment is the deliverable. The
[appearance contract](native-appearance-contract.json) describes that boundary.
The reviewed Novice and Oathkeeper native boards show Female and Male still poses.
The current [Verdict race-fit receipt](verdict-race-fit-current.json) and board
show all seven native appearances in a three-quarter pose without an obvious
face, chest, tabard or boot intersection in that sample.

These observations do not cover all six sets on all seven appearances. The
[current Novice combat receipt](novice-combat-studio-current.json) is three
single-frame observations. Earlier attack, received-hit and death work pins
earlier meshes and cannot accept current motion. The next useful art verification
is current equipment in representative attack, hit and death poses, plus the
remaining set/appearance fit combinations. A discovered visible fault should
drive a bounded asset correction; a missing capture alone is not an art defect.

The user's positive response to the revised Novice direction is retained as
directional feedback. It is not broadened here into approval of every later set,
every pose or every appearance. Save/reload validation does not change these
visual evidence limits. [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) remains the overall beta
record.

## Checks performed

- Equipment validator: all 18 original static GLBs and source manifests passed.
- Apparel validator: 18 skinned apparel assets and six rigid helmets passed.
- Package validator: all 39 rows, six-family progression, original asset hashes,
  renderer paths and references passed.
- Mercy, Censure and Verdict final source-review geometry hashes matched the
  current corresponding GLB, source and piece files.

The retained boards are local evidence. Their hashes provide identity, not a
substitute for public screenshots or native animation coverage.
