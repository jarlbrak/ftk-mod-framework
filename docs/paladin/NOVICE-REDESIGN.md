# Novice visual redesign

The user rejected the first novice set as too bulky and selected Classic WoW's
[Lightforge 3D viewer](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/transmog-set=595/lightforge-armor#modelviewer)
as the leading visual reference. The
[Lawbringer viewer](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/transmog-set=575/lawbringer-armor#modelviewer)
was also reviewed. This supersedes the novice set's earlier blue/steel direction.

The new original design uses a fitted crimson breastplate, restrained warm gold
trim, a defined waist, small layered shoulder plates, slim greaves and an
open-face red cap with a modest gold brow. The order keeps its own split-diamond
seal. Armor follows the body instead of adding thick volumes at every joint.
The starter set has fewer ornaments than the later progression sets.

The hammer keeps its tested grip and head positions while reducing head width
and depth, simplifying the end bands and removing the large pommel ornament.
The shield becomes a thin heater shape with a red field, narrow gold rim and
small central seal. Both weapon paths receive the revision.

All geometry and textures remain newly authored. The reference is viewed for
visual direction; no WoW surface data is imported into the package.

Review uses the original exported sources at matching scale, camera and light:
front, side and three-quarter comparisons for both character appearances, plus
separate weapon studies. Offline comparison is followed by a fresh in-game
preview, inventory and combat check. Historical art acceptance applies only to
the historical assets; the redesigned novice set requires its own acceptance.

## Hammer references

The actual Classic model viewers were inspected for
[Verigan's Fist](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=6953/verigans-fist#modelviewer),
[Ironfoe](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=11684/ironfoe#modelviewer),
[Sulfuras](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=17182/sulfuras-hand-of-ragnaros#modelviewer)
and [Might of Menethil](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=22798/might-of-menethil#modelviewer).
The user requested recognizable copies of famous hammer designs with newly authored assets.

The novice two-handed mesh follows Verigan's waisted silver barrel and paired
gold bands. The one-handed mesh follows Ironfoe's rectangular steel frame,
recessed bronze field and angular gold rune. Grip stations remain compatible
with the existing mounts. Crimson grips coordinate with the selected Lightforge
armor. The Mercy and Verdict source generators now use Sulfuras's red/gold spiked head and Menethil's
split hooked steel head around luminous blue wells. These later revisions also require fresh offline and native validation.
