# Paladin equipment progression

Paladin 2.0.1 candidate rules for framework 1.8.0. Native effect acceptance is pending. The [package content](../../marketplace/packages/paladin/content.json) defines the exact values. Native observations and their limits are recorded in [Validation](VALIDATION.md).

## Families and armor thresholds

Only the Head, Body and Foot slots count toward a family role. Two matching armor pieces grant that family's small theme. All three matching armor pieces grant its core role and its costs, regardless of the hands or accessories. One matching two-handed hammer with an empty offhand, or a matching one-handed hammer and shield, adds the family's armament completion benefit. A foreign weapon or shield cannot grant a second family role or completion, but it does not erase a completed armor core. Trinkets and necklaces never advance these thresholds. Non-Guardian wearers receive ordinary item stats and actions, not the class's Guardian role effects.

| Family | Two matching armor | Three matching armor core | Matching armament completion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mercy | Small healing improvement | Strong healing and magic Smite, with weaker physical offense, lower Guard reduction, an immediate Guard heal and a one-use focused class Smite healing bond | Raise the landed bonded Smite heal from 12% to 15% |
| Verdict | Small retaliation theme | Stronger durability and ally protection with lower damage, healing and initiative | Prevent the reviewed debuffs on a qualifying guarded direct attack |
| Censure | Small physical improvement | Guard mitigation readies +50% on one eligible single-target physical attempt, with weaker healing, Smite, Guard and personal defenses | Increase the conditional physical payoff against Censure's Armor outcome |

Mercy scales both healing percentages by its core healing multiplier. At 150% healing, immediate Guard heals 6% of that ally's maximum HP before flooring and missing-health limits. Guard arms one focused Paladin class Smite heal, 12% on a landed hit or 15% with matching armament. A qualifying attempt spends the bond even on a miss; it expires at the end of the next own turn. Censure's passive physical profile is 100%; an actual Guard-reduced direct enemy hit arms one nonstacking +50% eligible single-target physical attempt through the end of the next own turn. A miss spends it. Kingsfall and this charge use the strongest 1.5 multiplier rather than multiplying. The source profiles supply exact provisional percentages. Their costs are inseparable from the core role. Censure and Smite belong to the Paladin class and require an eligible equipped hammer. Mercy strengthens baseline Smite; it does not unlock it. Verdict's ward belongs to armament completion, not a freely mixable shield bonus. Censure's payoff is conditional on its own Armor outcome; it does not trigger for every incidental hit or damage-over-time tick. The three Artifacts have no set affinity, so they can accompany a three-armor core but cannot complete its armament.

## Acquisition

Novice, Oathkeeper and Highward pieces retain ordinary native loot and merchant declarations at native item levels 0, 1-2 and 3. Native item level is distinct from character or enemy display level. All 24 ordinary final-family pieces are exclusive to the equipment exchange catalog: each of Mercy, Verdict and Censure contributes Head, Body, Foot, 1H hammer, shield, 2H hammer, trinket and necklace. Their ordinary random-drop and merchant flags are disabled. Existing owned equipment is not confiscated.

The shared **Guild Token** is a physical, transferable resource owned by one character's inventory. The Paladin package is the first catalog using it; future classes may use the same currency and exchange service. No Paladin is required in the party to receive a token. A native admitted enemy reward with displayed combat level at least 8 offers a 10% ordinary chance, or a 50% chance for reviewed named-boss groups. The sixth consecutive eligible opportunity without a token guarantees one. A boss receives its boss chance in place of the ordinary chance. Multiple admitted enemies are processed in stable order, and multipart bosses count once per reviewed group. The chance and guarantee are provisional pacing rules, not measured campaign supply.

Each eligible native town's Back Alley catalog uses the native vendor interface and offers one chosen item for one token. Its balance badge shows the buying character's Guild Tokens. The visible stock contains only that character's class gear and excludes pieces already in their backpack or equipped slots. Purchases disappear from stock immediately, and confirmation rechecks class and ownership before spending a token. Buy opens a native no-refund confirmation and delivers the item to the backpack; equip it through the normal inventory menu. Three matching armor pieces cost three tokens; adding a matching 2H hammer costs one more, while the 1H-and-shield route costs two more. Accessories cost tokens but do not progress the set. The purchase has no reverse exchange or refund offer. The source candidate includes solo exchange behavior; co-op transaction and loot validation remain open gates. Native ordinary resale is a separate behavior and is not claimed as a token refund. See the [native content audit](../../scratch/paladin-polish/mechanics-gamefree/token-native-content-v4.md) for exact identity evidence and census limits.

## Slot and action coverage

| Route | Ordinary | Artifacts |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1H hammer, 2H hammer, shield | Six in each slot | One in each slot |
| Body, Head, Foot, Trinket, Neck | Six in each slot | None |

A 2H hammer excludes a shield. Every Paladin hammer has only the native single-target basic attack, labeled Strike, with a 1.0 regular-hit coefficient before normal stats, armor-role modifiers and other native rules. The explicit empty special-action list removes inherited splash, Shockwave and Stun across all 14 hammers, including both Artifacts. The native white outlined weapon icon remains. Other classes can use these hammers without receiving Censure or Smite.

The Paladin class grants Censure and Smite only while an eligible hammer is equipped. One-handed hammers select the one-handed Censure variant; two-handed and paired hammers select the two-handed variant. Eligibility uses exact registered IDs: the 14 Paladin hammers and native Smith, Toy, War, Ice, Great, Royal and Heavy Hammers plus the paired Hammers. Maces, axes, clubs and unrelated blunt weapons do not qualify. Native hammers retain their original weapon actions. All trinkets and necklaces now grant stats only. Mercy's armor core improves Smite without gating its availability. Armor and accessory modifiers remain intrinsic item stats. The set rules replace the earlier strongest-value union of individually equipped Guardian perks for the final families.

The new class still starts with Novice Hammer and Novice Aegis only. Armor, accessories and advanced hands must be earned or bought. The Artifacts retain native late-drop, night-market and dungeon-merchant declarations, with no ordinary town stock; see [Artifacts](ARTIFACTS.md). The final-family exchange rules do not put Artifacts in the token catalog.

## Evidence and release boundary

The previous macOS trial registered the earlier 51 equipment pieces and observed selected views. It did not verify these set thresholds or shared token acquisition. Required native checks include every armor combination, foreign hands, 1H-to-2H swaps, artifact substitution, non-Guardian wearers, displayed-level 7/8 boundaries, multipart bosses, collection and save/reload, town access, and exact chosen purchases. Balance needs matched fights for neutral, each core, each completed set and strongest mixed/artifact loadouts, with Focus, damage, healing, prevention, losses and turns recorded separately.

Website impact: update the Paladin guide and projected item cards from the verified 2.0.1 archive only after public release. The current public site remains tied to published 2.0.0.

Smite uses native magic damage and Resistance. Its baseline coefficient is 0.50 of equipped hammer damage; Mercy's three-armor core raises it to 0.80 before other modifiers. A perfect roll can trigger its authored native 25% stun chance, subject to immunity and hit outcome. The corrected unreleased row explicitly disables inherited Resistance bypass. Bounded R26 native encounters observed a zero-damage Resistance block, both a damaging non-proc and one new stun on an initially unstunned Ogre, and a Mercy Smite after the Paladin's own Censure Resistance mark. R25 observed damage without stun against an immune Wisp. Prior-byte R21 positive partial hits are relevant only through source equivalence; see [Validation](VALIDATION.md#unreleased-smite-damage-and-stun). These diagnostic fights do not establish proc frequency, campaign balance or full equipment-set acceptance.
