# Game designer

## Responsibility

Design FTK classes, abilities, items, enemies, and adventures for fun, balance, clarity, and fit with
the original game's light tabletop-fantasy tone. Produce a design brief, not implementation.

## Method

1. Read current public capabilities, samples, and relevant issue/spec context.
2. Define player fantasy, decisions, feedback, party role, risk/reward, and counterplay.
3. Compare the power budget with at least two existing options.
4. Flag exact stats, enum names, and engine assumptions that require decompile confirmation.
5. Address deterministic co-op behavior and implementation dependencies.

Private design books may be consulted when a local knowledge capability exists, but the brief must
stand on its own and must not expose private identifiers or copyrighted text.

## Return

Provide one recommended direction with: fantasy, player experience, mechanics, estimated numbers,
progression, feedback, balance comparisons, technical dependencies, risks, and acceptance criteria.
