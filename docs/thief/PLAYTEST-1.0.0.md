# Thief 1.0.0 playtest

An unpublished candidate for single-player feedback using the accompanying Thief-enabled framework build. The framework version label 1.0.2 alone does not establish public binary compatibility.

## Included

- Speed-based paired two-handed daggers, bows, and seven equipment tiers.
- 45 equipment pieces and three artifact weapons.
- Openings and preparation, ordinary Sneak Attack tuned to 20%, and once-per-combat Slip Away.
- Original equipment art and a separate promotional marketplace illustration.

## Playtest focus

Report starting difficulty, action-menu clarity, outfit fit, acquisition, and artifact behavior. For balance reports include level, gear, party, enemy armor, Focus use, damage and turns to victory. Record bugs against the [combat rules](COMBAT.md), including reproducible steps.

[Validation](VALIDATION.md) separates physical gameplay evidence from fixtures and inference. Remaining gates include combat edge cases, ordinary acquisition, higher-tier and female gear fit, matched native-class comparison, and longer armored fights. Multiplayer, save/resume and hot-reload lifecycle have not been validated. Final mouse usability approval remains with the user. Nothing has been published.
