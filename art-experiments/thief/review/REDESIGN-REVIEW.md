# Superseded raised-hood redesign comparison

The selected compact cap/cowl review is in `../redesign-review/README.md`. This page retains the superseded raised-hood comparison.

The historical fitting images in `README.md` show art the user rejected. The new original geometry is authored in `../ranged-apparel/redesign.py`, with progression exports, palette and icons in that directory. Weapon work is recorded by its separate asset manifests.

Native captures in `redesign/` show the existing Treasure Hunter male avatar with the installed original equipment and native body, face, hair and backpack retained. The Nightblade outfit is a disposable starting-item fixture. These studio images preserve native poses and use bone-based framing. They establish the appearance in the sampled native pose, not complete animation or gameplay acceptance.

## Nightblade raised hood, revision 6

The new vest, straps, belt, pouches, bracers and boots replace the plain prior shapes. The raised hood has a recessed face aperture, narrow crown and rear cloth folds. The back slit seen in earlier prototypes is resolved. The side remains broad and has small scalp/ear breakthroughs, so this is a comparison candidate rather than accepted final art. A separate compact cap and lowered cowl prototype is being evaluated without replacing these exports.

## Remaining evidence

Current female and alternate race fit, every other progression tier, ordinary attack/hit motion, equipment rebuilds, culling and loot displays require separate observations. Static GLB and icon validation does not establish those gates. Native material lighting can differ from the exported icon render; the captures are the authority for in-game color.
